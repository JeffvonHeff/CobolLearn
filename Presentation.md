Cobol Programming Language
=====================================
Cobol is a high-level programming language primarily used in business, finance, and administrative systems for companies and governments. It was designed for readability and ease of use in business applications.

Pros
------------
- Coding is straightforward and easy to read.
- Speed and efficiency in processing large volumes of data.
- Strong support for business applications and legacy systems.
- High reliability and stability in mission-critical applications.

Cons
------------
- Verbose syntax can lead to lengthy code.
- Limited modern programming features compared to newer languages.
- Declining community and support as newer languages gain popularity.
- Steep learning curve for new developers unfamiliar with its structure.

Use Cases
------------
- Business applications, especially in finance and banking.
- Legacy system maintenance and modernization.
- Data processing and batch jobs.
